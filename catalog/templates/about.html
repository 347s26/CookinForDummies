CookinForDummies helps you organize what you have at home, learn practical cooking techniques, save your favorites, and discover recipes that match your time and ingredients.

Home

The Home page is your starting point. Use it to enter ingredients you already have in your household, plus the utilities and tools available in your kitchen.

  • Add ingredients you currently have in your pantry, fridge, or freezer.
  • Track available kitchen utilities and tools before choosing a recipe.
  • Get a quick overview of what meals are realistic with your current setup.

Skills

The Skills page helps you learn new cooking skills step by step, so you can build confidence and improve your cooking techniques over time.

  • Learn foundational skills like chopping, sautéing, and seasoning.
  • Practice techniques that make recipes faster, safer, and more consistent.
  • Build cooking confidence whether you are a beginner or improving basics.

My Recipes

My Recipes is your personal collection of saved recipes, making it easy to come back to meals you like and plan what to cook next.

  • Save recipes you want to revisit later.
  • Keep favorite meals in one place for quick access.
  • Use saved recipes to plan your week and reduce decision fatigue.

Search

Use Search to find recipes you already know or explore new ones. You can browse current options, discover ideas, and quickly find meals that fit your needs.

  • Search for recipes by name, time, or other criteria.
  • Look up familiar recipes or discover new meal ideas.
  • Compare options quickly to pick the best recipe for the day.

Help

Need help getting started? Use these quick tips to navigate the app and find what you need faster.

  • Start on Home to review featured recipes and skill suggestions.
  • Use Search when you want to filter recipes by name, time, or rating.
  • Use My Recipes to revisit meals you saved previously.
  • Visit Skills to learn techniques before trying harder recipes.
  • If a page seems outdated, refresh your browser to load the latest changes.
