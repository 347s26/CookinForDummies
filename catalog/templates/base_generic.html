Quick Start Guide

Start here to get the most out of CookinForDummies:

  1. Use Search to find recipes by title, time, and rating.
  2. Open a recipe and click Save to build your personal list.
  3. Visit My Recipes anytime to view your saved meals.
  4. Go to Skills to learn techniques before trying tougher recipes.
